# Zocial 1.13.0

> **Draft — work in progress.** Rolls up the `1.12.x` development line (everything since the **1.12.0** release). New work lands as internal `1.12.1`, `1.12.2`, … dev patches and ships together here as `1.13.0`. Finalise the date and summary when the release is cut.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Quote posts from Mastodon now show inline (from 1.12.1).** Mastodon (4.4 and later) sends quotes in a different format than Akkoma/Pleroma, which Zocial didn't understand, so quoted posts from Mastodon servers were missing entirely. They now appear embedded in the post, just like on Akkoma.
- **"Reply to …" header shows reliably (from 1.12.3).** The line above a reply that shows who it answers used to appear only if Zocial happened to have the original post stored, so the same reply sometimes showed it and sometimes didn't. It now appears for replies to yourself, replies to you, and replies to anyone Zocial has seen before, and it loads faster.

---

## 🐛 Fixes & robustness

- **Word filters now hide images too (from 1.12.1).** A filter set to "hide with a warning" collapsed the post's text, but its images and videos were still shown in full. They are now blurred as well; tap the image to reveal it. This applies even with **Settings → General → Show sensitive media by default** turned on, because a filter is your own explicit choice.
- **Filters also apply to quoted posts (from 1.12.1).** A quoted post matching one of your filters was shown in full inside a post that didn't match. It is now collapsed behind the "Filtered" warning, including filters set to "hide completely", since a quoted post can't be removed on its own.
- **Posts quoting someone you muted or blocked disappear from your timelines (from 1.12.1).** Before, the quote itself silently went missing, and the post was left without its context. Now the whole post is hidden from your home, list and public timelines, the same way that person's own posts are. You still see it in notifications, threads and on profiles. Requires Mastodon 4.5 or later, which tells Zocial about muted and blocked quotes.
- **Quotes no longer vanish without a trace (from 1.12.1).** When a quote can't be shown (for example, it's still pending approval or was deleted), the "RE: link" to the quoted post now stays in the text instead of being removed.
- **Editing or redrafting a Mastodon quote post no longer fails (from 1.12.1).**
- **No more error flood when posts are edited (from 1.12.2).** When a post in your timeline was edited, Zocial could log the same "Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'id')" error again and again, and the edit wasn't saved locally. Favouriting, boosting or bookmarking that post could also fail with an error message, even though the server had accepted it. The cause was the local post cache remembering "not found" results.
- **No stray error when the emoji set is briefly unreachable while the emoji picker is open (from 1.12.2).** The emoji picker's own background update check is now handled like the rest of the emoji lookups (see 1.12.0).
